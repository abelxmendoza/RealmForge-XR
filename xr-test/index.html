Quest 2 passthrough test

Checks passthrough, true-scale grabbing, snapping to real surfaces and anchors before we rely on them in RealmForge. Same page runs on Quest 2 and Quest 3S; see docs/quest2-testing.md.

  • Point + trigger (or pinch) to grab an object.
  • Bring it near a real table, floor or wall: a green ghost shows where it will snap.
  • Release to place; it is then anchored to that spot.
  • Grip respawns the objects in front of you.
